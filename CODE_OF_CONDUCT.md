# Code of Conduct

Praxis follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its research community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without
explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository maintainer listed in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).
Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or limit
participation when needed to keep the project respectful and focused.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
